# Handoff: live voice latency

One live CoreS3 turn completed through the existing proxy. The measurement
started after the Mac received the audio. Total time was 52.1 seconds.
Listening took 9.8 seconds. Searching took 8.2 seconds. Comparing took 25.8
seconds. The final response took another 8.3 seconds after the pick phase.
These are phase timings from one turn, not individual model timings.

The backend runs transcription, routing, search, comparison, selection, checks,
and Gemini TTS. It generates TTS for every audio input even when `include_tts`
is false. The Mac proxy discards that audio and speaks the text locally.
Factual pick questions also fall through to the full search path.
